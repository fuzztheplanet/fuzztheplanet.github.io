Welcome to my little patch of internet!

This is where I spend my days collecting pesky insects, sweet-talking ciphers, pondering questionable life decisions and arguing with Emacs as if we were in a long-term relationship neither of us can leave. In the midst of all this, I try to remain a humble student of the Zen of pwn, seeking glimpses of enlightenment through poorly-documented code and salted butter caramel.

Visitors be warned, this blog is merely a patchwork of rants, experiments and unfinished work, written in plain language. ./posts hosts sporadic, one-off topics while the ./notes section contains longer pieces that might get updated over time (no promises, but optimism is free). Particularly shiny resources are hoarded in ./links. Further information about this website and contact details can be found under ./about. What Emacs allows me to share of my life resides on my ./now page.

With this out of the way, I wish you happy reading!
