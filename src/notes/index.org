#+TITLE: Various notes

Installation notes for /ArchLinux/-based [[./archlinux-workstation.org][workstations]] and [[./archlinux-vps.org][VPS]].

/[[https://github.com/fuzztheplanet/iota][iota]]/ is a R7RS Scheme interpreter written in Haskell. I followed [[https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Write_Yourself_a_Scheme_in_48_Hours][Write
Yourself a Scheme v1]] and [[https://www.wespiser.com/writings/wyas/home.html][v2]] back in the days and had it all revamped by AI. It's
neat and works quite well though. The source is on [[https://github.com/fuzztheplanet/iota][github]] and the documentation
is hosted [[https://fuzztheplanet.github.io/iota][here]].

/[[https://github.com/fuzztheplanet/microcosm][microcosm]]/ is a 64k demo engine. As an experiment, I wanted to see what
accessible AI could deliver for a computer without a mainstream graphic card. By
pushing claude and codex, I created a demo engine and a /hello world/ demo that
worked on my Intel UHD 620. Here are the [[https://github.com/fuzztheplanet/microcosm][code]] and [[https://fuzztheplanet.github.io/microcosm][documentation]]. A MP4 1080p
video (> 300MB) of the /Hello World/ demo can be downloaded [[https://github.com/fuzztheplanet/microcosm/releases/latest/download/hello-world-1080p60.mp4][here]].
